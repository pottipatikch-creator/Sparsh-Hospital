How to avail the benefits

Please carry the required employee identification

At the hospital

Present your employee ID

Employees must present their official Swiggy Employee ID Card at the hospital registration or billing desk to avail the benefits.

The discounts are applicable directly at the time of billing.
Employee eligibility

Access while actively employed

Since employees avail this benefit using their official Swiggy Employee ID Card, access to these exclusive corporate discounts will be lost upon exit from Swiggy and the surrender of the ID card.

For any ongoing long-term treatments at the time of exit, employees must coordinate directly with SPARSH Hospital regarding the transition to standard billing rates.
